Audience and job: general public with a Last.fm history, power users in the same product. Task: build and read playlist automations.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One ember ribbon of dithered pixels is the site's only ornament and it never restarts: it travels between pages and changes shape, angle and heat. Refuses the category default of a sidebar, cover-art cards and a neutral dark shell.

OWN-WORLD: Ink black ground, warm cream text, the Braise ember ramp for the ribbon, amber for actions. Square controls, 4px pixel grid. Tiny5 for display and numerals, Jersey 20 for everything read closely, both from `--font-display` and `--font-text`, sized on their pixel grid (`src/index.css`), no letter-spacing. No cards, hairlines separate rows.

STORY: The visitor knows where everything is before noticing the world, then sees their listening shaping the ribbon. They set a recipe, read the preview, and leave.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with logo, links and a sliding dotted marker, account on the right. Content in plain blocks on the ground. The ribbon roams wherever content is not and thins into pixels near it. Primary action in amber.

FORM: Owner-pinned pixel dither over the roll, validated on a playable prototype. Signature move: the ribbon morphs instead of remounting. seed: 5cbda9a77c3edb33

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md (written from the Creator), and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
