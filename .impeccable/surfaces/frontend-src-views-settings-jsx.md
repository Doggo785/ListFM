Scope: Settings and every screen before the site; visitor mode Operate.
Audience and job: anyone with a Last.fm history. Task: get in, connect Last.fm, then change a setting and leave.

## Direction contract

THESIS: These screens are the same world as the rest of the site, reached by the same ribbon. The ribbon's continuity between pages comes first, in both directions.

FORM: Auth screens put the forged name on the ribbon and one task beside it. Settings are hairline rows on the left with the ribbon down the free side. No cards, no decoration of their own.
