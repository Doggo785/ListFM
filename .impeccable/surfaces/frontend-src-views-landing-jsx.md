Scope: the page before the site, the only one that sells. Mode: Brand. Chosen by the owner on 2026-11-06 from a playable prototype of three variants: "The forge".

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the machine. A living thread shows what it does with one real person's real history, keeps going by itself, then turns to the visitor. Nobody has to read to understand it.

OWN-WORLD: Braise palette on ink, Tiny5 titles, Jersey 20 text, the ribbon's dither on its 4px grid, no radius, no shadow, no gradient, no glow. The thread is a presence with no face and no voice: "the thread", in the third person, never a mascot. It stays the site's sober ribbon, with no flames: only a forged name burns, and the thread's pixels fall.

STORY: It wakes, it listens (the weeks since July 2024, the total), it works (Window, Filters, Sort, Limit, with real counts), it delivers a cover, it keeps going with nobody touching anything, it obeys other recipes, it asks for the visitor's name, and it leads to the button along the plain truths: playlists leave by hand, Last.fm is required, the first load takes minutes.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Black. A pixel breathes under where the name will be, then runs right as a fuse: the name exists only where it has passed, and burns from cold iron to cream. The promise comes in as dither. "Describe a playlist once. It keeps coming."

FORM: One pinned scroll for scenes 2 to 6, then two plain sections. The scroll drives everything and never hijacks the wheel; the thread lives on its own over it. Signature interaction: each track of a real month is one square that falls and sorts as the visitor scrolls, backwards too. The visitor's own artist in the forge is the page's climax.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; a complete page under reduced motion, no second version on phones.
