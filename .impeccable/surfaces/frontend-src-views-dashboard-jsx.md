Scope: the Dashboard, the page people land on. Mode: Experience above the fold, Operate for the runs and the links.
Audience and job: anyone with a Last.fm history comes back to see themselves in their listening, and to check what ran.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The Dashboard is a poster of your listening right now, and your year played like a track. The ribbon stops being an ornament here: its width is your listens, day by day. Refuses the stat tiles, the greeting and the grid of cards.

OWN-WORLD: Braise on ink, Tiny5 and Jersey 20, no radius, no shadow. The same dithered ribbon as every page, fed with daily listens, with flame at its edge and embers leaving it. Album art in original quality. Letters heated like metal.

STORY: The visitor sees who they are listening to, sees the name catch fire from the ribbon, reads the latest runs, then scrolls and rewinds the year: each month's top artist with their own album, flying in from the left. Or switches to their automations on the same arc.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Giant artist name in Tiny5, left-aligned, as large as it fits. Under it the ribbon across the full width. Below, one line of context, the shelf of top albums, the latest runs on the right, totals at the bottom. No primary button: the only action is reading.

FORM: Poster over a pinned deck, owner-chosen mix of positions 1 and 2 on the structure round. seed:16f41760d2081065. Signature: the forge (ignition from the ribbon's playhead) and the flight (scroll rewinds the year, covers fly on a 3D arc through the flame).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
